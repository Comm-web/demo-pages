Choose a Table Configuration for Your Insert Molding Process

The table configuration determines where inserts are loaded and finished parts are removed. The examples below are starting points for evaluation. Final selection depends on your mold, insert retention method, handling time and production layout.

Fixed Bottom Platen

When to evaluate it: Parts with straightforward loading access, such as individual metal inserts or small assemblies. A fixed platen can also be evaluated for trials and production runs that do not require a separate loading station.

Loading and automation: The lower mold stays in the molding position. Inserts are placed and parts are removed while the mold is open. Manual or robotic handling depends on access and the selected machine.

Selection limits: There is no separate table station for parallel loading. Include insert placement and part removal time when evaluating the complete cycle.

View fixed platen models: V · VC · VH.

Slide Table

When to evaluate it: Projects where careful insert placement is easier at a separate loading position, such as terminals, plug inserts or connectors.

Loading and automation: The lower mold moves to an accessible position for loading and unloading, then returns for molding. Review the slide travel against the operator workstation or robot reach.

Selection limits: Allow space for table movement and handling equipment. A single slide table does not automatically provide simultaneous loading and molding.

View slide table models: V-S · VH-S.

Double Shuttle Table

When to evaluate it: Repeated insert molding operations where placing several inserts or removing parts occupies a significant portion of the cycle, such as multi-insert connectors.

Loading and automation: Lower mold positions alternate between loading and molding. A suitable tooling arrangement and control sequence can allow handling at one position to overlap with molding at the other. Review access to both loading positions and coordination with insert feeding or part removal equipment.

Selection limits: Confirm the lower mold arrangement, alignment, temperature control connections and table travel. The cycle benefit depends on the balance between handling time and molding time.

View double shuttle models: V-SD · VC-SD · VH-SD.

Rotary Table

When to evaluate it: Repetitive insert molding with a consistent loading sequence, such as connector or sensor components, particularly when planning an organized production cell.

Loading and automation: The table indexes mold positions between the molding area and handling stations. Inserts are loaded at a handling station and moved to the molding position. Evaluate feeders, locating fixtures, part removal and inspection against the station layout.

Selection limits: Confirm station count, table diameter, load capacity, mold arrangement and utility routing. Output depends on molding, handling and indexing times.

View rotary table models: V-R · VC-R · VH-R · All-Electric V3E-R-55T.

What to Check Before Selecting a Vertical Injection Molding Machine

Clamping force is one selection factor. Prepare these details for a specification review.

Requirement Information to prepare
Material and shot requirements Resin grade, plastic weight per shot including runners, cavity count and required injection pressure or speed.
Mold compatibility Mold length, width, thickness and weight; opening clearance; mounting and nozzle connection details.
Insert handling Insert dimensions, quantity per shot, orientation, locating method and loading or removal time.
Production target Required output, target cycle time, operating hours and expected changeovers.
Cell layout Available floor space, working height, table travel and access for operators or robots.
Drive and automation Energy measurement criteria, production environment requirements, insert feeding, part removal and inspection needs.

When comparing shot capacity, check the reference material used in the specification. A capacity stated for polystyrene should be reviewed against your actual resin and shot requirements.

Vertical Injection Molding Machine FAQ

How do I choose between a double shuttle table and a rotary table?

Compare your loading sequence, mold arrangement and available space. A double shuttle arrangement alternates positions along a linear path; a rotary table indexes positions around a circular path. Both require review of handling time and coordination with molding.

Will a moving table reduce cycle time?

It can reduce waiting when insert loading or part removal overlaps with molding at another station. The result depends on the table configuration, tooling and time required for each operation.

Does a rotary table require full automation?

A rotary table can be evaluated for manual handling or an automated cell. Confirm station access, the operating sequence and compatibility with the selected machine.
